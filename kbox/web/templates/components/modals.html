How to Use kbox

Adding Songs

  1. Type a song or artist in the search box and tap "Search" (kbox adds "karaoke" for you)
  2. Tap a result to open the confirmation dialog
  3. Optionally change the key (pitch) to suit your voice
  4. Tap "Add to Queue"

Not sure what to sing? "✨ Suggest for me" (when it's available) offers ideas based on what you've sung and tonight's theme.

Favorites and History

Tap the ⭐ on a search result to save a song to your favorites, then open ⭐ at the top of the page to find it again. The 🕘 button lists songs you've sung before so you can queue them again quickly.

Queue

Your songs appear in the queue with a yellow You tag and their title. Other guests' song titles stay hidden so the next song is a surprise. Numbers show the order of upcoming songs, and the currently playing song is highlighted in blue. When you're up next, a bar at the top of the page lets you know.

Editing Your Songs

Tap a song you added to change its key, replace it with a different version (🔁 Replace Song), or remove it. Other guests' songs can't be opened.

Key (Pitch) Adjustment

You can change the key when adding a song or by editing it later. Range: -12 to +12 semitones (one octave down/up), with 0 being the original key. While your own song is playing, tap "🎚 Adjust key" on the Now Playing card to change it live.

Your Name

The line at the top shows the name you're singing as. Tap "Not you?" to switch, for example if you're sharing a phone. Songs already in the queue stay under the previous name.
